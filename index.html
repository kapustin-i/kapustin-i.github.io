The Bush Book: a consciously bilingual human–machine book

Concept note and map of the bush · v0.1 · 2026 · EN / RU

The text of our time is already bilingual: living human words and computed machine words are woven together, and a person and a machine both write and read it. The Bush Book is built on this consciously — each chapter shows its seam, carries a passport for the machine reader, and records its sources and outflows. Its chapters come out now as articles.

The Second Generation of AI · Второе поколение ИИ

Essay · v1.0 · 2026 · EN / RU · written with ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek

The smarter the model, the harder it is to spot its error. What matters in new AI is not power or speed, but “first, do no harm”: the first generation extends a person, the second extends and protects — catching errors before the signature reaches people. The pilot must come home. Grows out of The Snake's Tongue.

Важна не мощь и не скорость новых ИИ, а «Не навреди». Первое поколение расширяет человека, второе — расширяет и защищает.
